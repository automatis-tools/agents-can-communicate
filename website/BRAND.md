# ACC brandbook

ACC connects independent coding sessions through questions, reviews and handoffs.
The identity makes that connection recognizable while keeping installation and
reading clear. White space, readable dark type and tactile blue/yellow objects
carry the same character across practical guides and technical evidence.

Bright yellow is a marker or background, never ordinary yellow text on white.
Use ink or amber with yellow surfaces. Keep titles in ink and inline links in
link blue; link hover adds color and underline. These measured color pairs
come from the implemented palette and do not imply a complete accessibility
certification. The site stays light, including commands and navigation overlays.

## Type and controls

Self-hosted Public Sans carries the wordmark, navigation, headings and interface.
Home display type is 650 weight at 46px desktop, 39px tablet and 34px mobile.
Body starts at 16px; captions and metadata are generally 13–14px. Research essay
body retains Georgia at 19px desktop and 21px mobile; commands use monospace.

Primary actions are brand blue with white text, a 44px minimum height and 7px
corners. Keyboard focus uses a visible 2px link-blue outline with 4px offset.
The shared shell reaches 1120px with 32px desktop and 20px mobile side gutters.

## Mark and asset use

Use the existing paired blue/yellow C mark with the ACC wordmark. The header
symbol is 52×34px; favicon and social artwork use the same flat semantic palette.
Preserve proportions and use the authored files rather than reconstructing them
with text or substituting a different connector symbol.

The home object and six editorial objects share thick rounded forms, softly
pebbled matte material, cobalt and sunny-yellow highlights/shadows, a three-quarter
studio view, transparent breathing room and a restrained contact shadow. Their
material colors have a deliberate light/shadow range; flat interface swatches
do not prescribe every rendered pixel. Preserve the original alpha and shading.
Do not recolor the assets or add glows, opaque panels, text or fake product UI.

The hero is a 900×600 WebP. The six editorial WebPs are 512×512 and approximately
20–33KB each. Keep their exact-prompt JSON sidecars alongside the shipping files.
Decorative images use empty alt text and hidden figure containers; they never
stand in for a product capture or evidence of client behavior.

## Composition

Illustrations belong to the content they illuminate. Preserve useful asymmetry,
white space and a clear reading order; size follows the object's role.

| Surface | Placement and desktop size |
| --- | --- |
| Guides | Route beside the steps, 165px |
| Integrations | Connector in receiving-method aside, 110px |
| Comparisons | Choice object inside workflow overview, 240px |
| Research | Magnifier in left archive margin, 210px |
| Reports | Paper stack beside closing evidence note, 145px |
| Docs | Book near quickstart commands, 96px |

Mobile recomposes each context: smaller guide art stays beside steps, comparison
action drops below its overview, research art precedes archive rows, and docs
commands gain the full width. Selected article art belongs in rails, identity,
method, metadata, provenance or reference areas; guide/docs mobile vignettes
close their manuscripts. Avoid a universal header image wrapper or repeated
upper-right stamp. Preserve each reading family's own type measure and tables.

## Voice and editorial boundaries

Lead introductions with concrete reader value: stop carrying messages between
windows, ask another model for a review, or continue work in another session. Use
confident, plain language and a clear action. Avoid opening with “small” or
“little,” enterprise sales language and invented dashboards.

Keep reference and evidence writing precise. Name sources, versions, dates,
methods and uncertainty; label examples as illustrative. Do not invent traction,
savings, endorsements, test results or client capabilities. Each session retains
its own model, context, permissions and lifecycle; ACC collects no raw transcripts.
A delivery receipt does not prove model attention.

Preserve all 22 manuscripts, article headings and anchors, canonical URLs,
metadata, structured data and internal links. Six index structures and six
reading layouts support eight metadata types; brand consistency must retain
those editorial distinctions. Keep install, search, compatibility and source
references easy to reach through shared navigation and ordinary links.
