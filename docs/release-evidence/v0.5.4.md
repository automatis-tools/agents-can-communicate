## Final gates and boundaries

The final full suite runs after the evidence commit, as required by
[Releasing](../RELEASING.md). The release pull request records its pre-push and
hosted CI results. The scoped checks above do not claim those later results.

Automatic service preparation requires macOS arm64, Codex >=0.154.0, an existing
matching managed standalone installation, and complete setup consent during an
explicit install. Client trust and a new session may still be required. Doctor,
hooks, automatic refresh, dry run, and refusal do not start the service. Custom
permission policies remain preserved. Existing installations retain their saved
incoming-delivery choices during upgrade.

[Development validation](../internal/evidence/complete-delivery-setup.md) records
the feature and macOS CI mutation checks. Its development archive and capture
remain historical evidence rather than being relabeled as this release.
