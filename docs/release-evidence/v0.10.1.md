## What changed since 0.10.0

One merged pull request changed the package; its development record is below this section of
the CHANGELOG:

- **#274 (#273).** `listNativeHolds` judges a binding without a client pid by its session
  record: it is no hold when the record is gone, names an exited process, or, with no process
  recorded, is offline by ACC's presence rule (closed, or 30 minutes without a heartbeat). A
  live recorded process, an unreadable record or a missing store keeps it a hold.
  `prepareRefresh` removes the stale ones under each session's lifecycle mutex, because an
  older generation's activator imports the pending generation's `prepareRefresh` before it
  lists blockers by its own rule.

## Upgrade from the published 0.9.1

The published `agents-can-communicate@0.9.1` was installed from npm into an isolated prefix,
with its own `HOME`, `ACC_DATA_HOME` and project, a `PATH` of the prefix, client stubs, node and
system directories, and a local registry that served this exact archive. Three runs:

- **A live 0.9.1 process.** A contract-6 store with an answered and an open question, and 0.9.1's
  own `acc-mcp` holding contract 6. `acc update` printed `ACC 0.10.1 is ready; waiting for 1
  active or unidentified process(es): PID 68287 (acc-mcp; store contract 6).` After the server
  exited, the next plain `acc status` activated 0.10.1; within 1.3 seconds the store was on
  contract 7, `store-upgrade.json` was complete and the 0.9.1 generation was reclaimed. Only
  `protocol.json`, a new `indexes/` and eight `stage/*.published` markers differed from the
  store copied before the update. 0.10.1 answered the open question on it. The installed
  generation matched the archive file for file, the Codex `config.toml` was byte for byte the
  one 0.9.1 wrote, and the registry saw three requests.
- **Stale bindings.** Two bindings without a pid that name sessions with no record, the shape
  0.8.5 wrote. `acc update` printed `Updated ACC to 0.10.1; integrations refreshed.`: the 0.9.1
  updater activated it by its own rule after the candidate's `prepareRefresh` removed both. The
  store moved to contract 7. The published 0.10.0, run the same way during #274, stayed
  pending behind the same two bindings.
- **A heartbeating session without a pid**, besides the two stale bindings. The two were
  removed; the third binding stayed and kept 0.10.1 pending, and the store stayed on contract 6.

## Real clients

On the maintainer's Mac (macOS arm64), the real-clients tests ran against the candidate commit's
packed tree, with an isolated home and a model stub on 127.0.0.1. Claude Code 2.1.292 and
Codex 0.160.1 attached through their own hooks, saw each other and carried a message into the
next turn, and an idle interactive Claude Code woke through its inbox. Both passed on the first
run.

The real-clients CI job runs the same two tests on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest` for every
pull request; both passed on the final head of #274.
