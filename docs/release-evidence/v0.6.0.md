## Final gates and retained evidence

Review on #182 found that the relay's handover to its detached `run` child had no error
listener on the child's stdin: a child that exited without reading its payload - the managed
launcher does, while a runtime update holds admission - made the write fail with EPIPE as an
unhandled stream error, and `start` would crash inside the agent's turn instead of printing
its one line. A test handing a real child more than a pipe buffer reproduced `write EPIPE`
before the correction and passes after it; a child that exits before its ready line now
fails at once instead of after ten seconds. The correction changed packed files, so the
earlier candidate (`835f1fd`, SHA-256 `7921df2d…`) was discarded and every check above was
repeated on this archive.

The full suite runs after candidate B through the unchanged pre-push hook. GitHub checks
cover Node 24 on Linux and macOS and package installation on both platforms; on PR #181
the Linux job failed once on `native-attempt-doctor`, a known load-sensitive test, and
passed on re-run.
